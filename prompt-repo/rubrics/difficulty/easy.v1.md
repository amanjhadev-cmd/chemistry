# Easy Difficulty Rubric

**Cognitive level:** Bloom's `remember` and `understand` only.

**Acceptable item shapes**
- Direct recall of a definition, formula, law, or named entity from the chapter.
- One-step calculation with all values given in the stem and no unit conversion.
- "Which of the following is the definition of X?" / "X is also known as ___."

**Reject if**
- The item requires combining two distinct concepts.
- The item involves multi-step reasoning, even if each step is trivial.
- The item assumes prior-chapter knowledge that wasn't restated.

**Distractors**
- Plausible synonyms or near-related terms from the same chapter.
- Common misreadings of the named entity (e.g. molarity vs molality).
