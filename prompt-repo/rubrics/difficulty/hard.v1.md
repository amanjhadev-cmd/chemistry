# Hard Difficulty Rubric

**Cognitive level:** Bloom's `apply`, `analyze`, `evaluate`.

**Acceptable item shapes**
- Multi-step reasoning combining 2-3 concepts from the chapter.
- 3+ step numerical with unit conversions and intermediate quantities.
- Assertion-Reason style requiring evaluation of causal claims.
- HOTS items: predict, justify, contradict, or design.
- Items where the surface form looks like one concept but the answer requires recognizing it's actually another.

**Reject if**
- Solvable by direct recall or single-formula substitution.
- Requires knowledge outside the named chapter.
- Has more than one defensible correct answer.

**Distractors**
- Results of dropping one step in a multi-step solution.
- Sign errors / order-of-magnitude errors that students commonly make.
- Plausibly-related concepts from adjacent chapters (must be subtle).
